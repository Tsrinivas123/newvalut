How VidVault Works

Download your favorite permissible media in four easy steps without bloat, popups, or redirects.

01

Paste Video URL

Copy any standard YouTube video, Shorts, or mobile link and paste it into the analyzer box.

02

Analyze Streams

Our backend extracts exact formats, verified resolutions, and audio bitrates using official engines.

03

Select Quality

Pick from progressive MP4 (1080p, 720p, 480p) or high-fidelity audio streams (M4A/MP3).

04

Instant Download

The server delivers the stream directly as a file attachment right to your browser with zero fake buttons.

Core Advantages

Engineered for Reliability

Built with Python, yt-dlp, and modern serverless standards for a fast, honest downloader experience.

Real Formats Only

Never see simulated 4K or 8K options that don't exist. VidVault strictly surfaces available streams returned by yt-dlp.

High Quality MP4

Download complete, standalone progressive MP4 formats that play smoothly across phones, Macs, and PCs without third-party codecs.

Crisp Audio Extraction

Save songs, podcasts, and speeches as pristine M4A or converted MP3 audio files with high bitrates.

Serverless Architecture

Powered by Vercel Serverless Functions with smart ephemeral memory management and auto-scaling performance.

Zero Logs & High Privacy

We do not store your download history or track URLs. Temporary download buffers are wiped immediately upon completion.

Fully Responsive

Engineered with a responsive dark interface that adapts seamlessly to desktop monitors, tablets, and smartphones.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding compatibility, format choices, and serverless download limits.

VidVault supports standard YouTube video links (`youtube.com/watch?v=...`), shortened links (`youtu.be/...`), YouTube Shorts (`youtube.com/shorts/...`), and embedded URLs. Private, password-protected, or age-restricted videos that require Google account login are not accessible.
Direct MP4 (progressive format) contains both video and audio together in a single stream. These download instantly with zero re-encoding or processing overhead. Higher resolutions (like 1080p) are often stored as separate video and audio tracks by YouTube, which require FFmpeg to merge together during download.
To operate reliably within Vercel's serverless execution timeouts and ephemeral temporary disk storage, VidVault supports videos up to 30 minutes in length and files up to 200MB. If a requested stream exceeds these bounds, a clear error message is returned rather than a stalled download.
Yes! VidVault offers native high-bitrate M4A (AAC) audio which downloads natively without conversion. In environments where FFmpeg is installed, an automatic 192 kbps MP3 conversion option is also provided.
No. VidVault is strictly stateless. We do not maintain user databases, track user IPs, or keep video logs. Temporary download files created during delivery are securely purged from the server immediately after your download begins.
